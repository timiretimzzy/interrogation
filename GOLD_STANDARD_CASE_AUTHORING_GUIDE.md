# Gold-Standard Case Authoring Guide

## Target experience

Build curiosity, uncertainty, suspicion, plausible false confidence, reinterpretation, cross-branch synthesis, recovery, an earned realization, and explanatory satisfaction. Players should connect independently discovered information, not merely collect it.

## Backward authoring and information layers

Begin with the canonical incident: culprit, motive, method, opportunity, complete timeline, character knowledge and ignorance, secrets, lies, physical and misleading evidence, and likely wrong theories. Work backward into discoverable routes.

Keep canonical truth, character knowledge, willingness, player discoveries, confirmed deductions, and player theory separate. A fact, clue, evidence item, claim, contradiction, deduction, suspicion, and theory have different jobs. Characters are never omniscient exposition machines; define each important person's role, knowledge, ignorance, secret, lie, motive, pressure point, willingness, corroboration, and accidental disclosure.

## Investigation design

Treat every question as a lead. Define its player motivation, hypothesis, possible response, progression purpose, availability, obsolescence, and closure. Use baseline inquiry, timeline verification, contradiction confrontation, evidence presentation, motive pressure, relationship probe, alibi test, redirect, and closure questions.

Every response needs voice, epistemic status, narrative purpose, progression effect, and eligibility relationship. Use truth, partial truth, evasion, lie, and admission deliberately. Critical progression must have reliable independent routes, never lucky weighting alone.

Cases may deepen from surface event through suspect theories, alibi contradictions, hidden relationships, motive/method reconstruction, cross-branch synthesis, central aha, and accusation. These are flexible layers, not a fixed count. Corroborate key conclusions through genuinely different testimony, evidence, timeline, behavior, or motive routes.

## Leverage, pacing, and inference

A false lead must plausibly explain something, fail elsewhere, close explicitly, and leave useful understanding. Contradictions require conflicting claims, exposing evidence, confrontation opportunity, and payoff. A lead must open, investigate, then progress, redirect, contradict, or close.

Use automatic deductions only for clear implications. Reserve at most one central player-triggered deduction for the meaningful cross-branch connection. It may confirm understanding, but never fill WHO/WHY/HOW or alter the theory board. Use disclosure requirements as OR-of-AND preparation routes so major truth does not collapse prematurely.

## Accusation and validation

Accusations test explanatory completeness, not trivia. For every dimension define the prompt, canonical answer, plausible alternatives, proof requirements, and a diagnostic that states what the choice fails to explain without revealing an unsupported answer. Run structural validation, state-space validation, adversarial simulation when available, narrative review, and human playtests.

## Workflow

1. Write canonical incident truth and complete timeline.
2. Model character knowledge and evidence.
3. Identify plausible false theories and accusation dimensions.
4. Work backward into critical facts and independent discovery routes.
5. Build the question network and response variants.
6. Add contradictions, deductions, disclosure requirements, and lead lifecycles.
7. Define proof and diagnostics.
8. Run mechanical validation and adversarial simulation.
9. Review narrative structure, human-playtest, then revise pacing and fairness.

## Avoid

Avoid omniscient NPCs, exposition dumps, lucky critical responses, silent/disappearing leads, fake choice, purposeless evidence, payoff-free contradictions, automatic case-solving deductions, theory auto-fill, trivia accusations, spoiler diagnostics, arbitrary gates, disguised linearity, unconverged branches, unresolved red herrings, unsupported twists, obvious culprits, impossible inferences, and mistaking validator success for fun.
